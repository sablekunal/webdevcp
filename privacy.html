Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This policy explains what Sportikcollects when you use the app or this website, and how it's used. It applies to the Sportik Android app and to sportik.in and its subdomains.

Information we collect

When you sign in to organize or join a tournament, we collect the account information provided by your sign-in method (such as your name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In), plus the tournament data you create - teams, players, matches, and results.

Public tournament pages (accessed via a share link or QR code) don't require sign-in and don't collect personal data from viewers beyond standard web server logs.

How we use it

Account and tournament data is used to operate the app: to show your tournaments, run the draw, generate fixtures, and display brackets and standings to anyone you share the tournament with. We don't sell personal data to third parties.

Where it's stored

Tournament and account data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider. Sign-in is handled by Google OAuth - we never see or store your Google password.

Analytics

This website uses privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics (Vercel Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic to pages like this one. It does not track you across other sites.

Your choices

You can delete a tournament you organize at any time from inside the app, which removes its teams, players, and matches. To delete your account entirely, contact us at hello@sportik.in.

Children's privacy

Sportik is intended for organizers and players old enough to register their own account and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page if this policy changes materially.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@sportik.in.
