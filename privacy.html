Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This policy explains what Sportik collects when you use the app or this website, and how it is used. It applies to the Sportik Android and web apps, sportik.in, and all subdomains.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to organize or manage a tournament, we collect the account information provided by your sign-in method (such as your name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In), plus the tournament data you create — teams, players, matches, venues, and scores.

For visitors, players, and spectators accessing shared tournament links or public tools, no login or account creation is required, and no personally identifying information is requested.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information strictly to provide and maintain the Sportik service: generating brackets, updating live points tables, synchronizing live draw results, and displaying tournament schedules.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your tournament data for third-party advertising networks.

3. Public Tournament Data

When you create a tournament and choose to share its public link or QR code, match fixtures, team names, brackets, and score results become viewable by anyone with the link. Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal details into public tournament fields.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encrypted storage and transmission protocols. We maintain automated backups and restrict administrative access to authorized operational personnel only.

5. Contact and Requests

If you wish to request deletion of your account, delete a tournament, or have any questions about this policy, please reach out to us at hello@sportik.in.
