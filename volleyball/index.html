Championship Architecture

League + Knockout Stage Progression

Directly aligned with official tournament banner rules: 4 Groups × 4 Teams | Single Round Robin | Top 1 Advances.

01

Group Stage (Round Robin)

16 teams partitioned into 4 distinct groups (Group A, B, C, D). Every squad plays 3 round-robin matches in their group, totaling 6 matches per group (24 matches total across Courts 1 & 2).

Win = 1 Pt | Draw = 0 Pts | Loss = 0 Pts
02

Top 1 Advances (4 Qualifiers)

Only the #1 group winner from each of the 4 groups earns a spot in the Knockout Stage: Winner Group A, Winner Group B, Winner Group C, and Winner Group D.

Exactly 4 Qualifiers Advance
03

Knockout Stage & Grand Final

Semi-Final 1 features Winner Group A vs Winner Group B. Semi-Final 2 features Winner Group C vs Winner Group D. The victors battle in the Grand Final for the Fr. Barco Memorial Trophy and ₹12,000 cash.

Regulations & Code of Conduct

Official 6-A-Side Throwball Rules

Standard Throwball Federation guidelines adapted for rapid memorial tournament format.

Team Composition

  • Exactly 6 active players on court at any time.
  • Maximum 3 rolling substitutes allowed on bench.
  • Girls-only category; all players must wear matching sports jerseys or bibs.

Catching & Release (3-Sec Rule)

  • Ball must be caught cleanly with both hands simultaneously.
  • Must be thrown with one hand above shoulder height.
  • Max 3-second hold time. Body touch or bobbling ball is a fault.

Match Scoring System

  • League: Best of 3 sets of 15 points (Rally scoring).
  • Finals: Best of 3 sets of 21 points with sudden death at 20-20.
  • Change of court sides after every completed set.

Net Violations & Service

  • Service must be taken from behind the baseline box within 5 seconds of referee whistle.
  • Net touch by any player or reaching over net is an immediate point to opponents.

Reporting & Identification

  • Teams must report 30 minutes before first designated match.
  • Carry valid School/College/Aadhaar ID for age & identity verification.
  • 15 minutes grace time before a match forfeiture is declared.

Referee Authority & Discipline

  • Referees appointed by St. Xavier's Youth sports council.
  • Referee's decision is final and binding on all match controversies.
  • Yellow/Red card disciplinary measures strictly enforced.
