What can I use the picker wheel for?

Use it for sports team draws, selecting batting/bowling sides, deciding volunteer duties, giveaway raffles, or order of play.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely client-side in your mobile or desktop browser without registration or server latency.

Can I pick multiple winners without repeats?

Yes — check "Remove winner on landing", and each winning entry is automatically eliminated from the pool after the spin.
