help Frequently Asked Questions

Is this coin toss actually random?

Yes. Each flip uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), giving heads and tails an equal 50/50 chance every time — identical to a physical coin.

Can both captains see the result at the same time?

Yes — hold the smartphone between both captains or share the screen so both sides observe the flip and result simultaneously.

Does the toss work the same way for every sport?

The coin flip is identical. In Cricket, the winning captain chooses whether to bat or bowl first. In Football or Throwball, the winner chooses the side of court or who serves first.

Can I use this instead of a physical coin?

Yes — it is an authorized and tamper-proof substitute when no coin is on hand or for settling match decisions fairly.
