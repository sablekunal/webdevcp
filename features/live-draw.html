Live Team Draw

The moment every organizer dreads doing fairly in front of a WhatsApp group - Sportik turns the team draw into a live, visible event anyone with the tournament link can watch.

  • Switch between Random and Manual mode per draw - spin teams into open slots automatically, or place each team by hand when seeding matters.
  • Every assignment updates in real time, so players watching the shared tournament link see the bracket fill in live.
  • Reassign a team into a different slot before the draw closes if you make a mistake.
  • A full history log records who was drawn into which slot and when, so there's no dispute after the fact.

Random vs. Manual draw

Use Random when every team should have an equal chance at any slot. Use Manual when you need to seed strong teams apart, or slot in a team that registered late. You can mix both in the same draw.
