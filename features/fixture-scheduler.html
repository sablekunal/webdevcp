Auto & Manual Fixture Scheduler

The part that usually takes an organizer an evening with a spreadsheet - Sportik's fixture wizard builds your entire match list in one pass, or hands you full manual control when you need it.

  • Pick a format and team count, and the scheduler generates every match for the whole tournament in one step - groups, knockout rounds, or both.
  • Prefer control? Switch to manual and lay out matches yourself, in any order, on any date.
  • A review step shows the full fixture list before anything is confirmed, so you can catch a mistake before players see it.
  • Re-generate is safe: changing the format later re-runs the wizard without corrupting results already entered.

Auto vs. Manual scheduling

Auto is fastest - pick a format and go. Manual is better when matches need to fit around ground availability, or when only some rounds are ready to be scheduled and the rest will follow later.
