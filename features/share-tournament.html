Share Tournament Instantly

A tournament only an organizer can see doesn't help players and fans on match day. Sportik gives every tournament a public, read-only link and QR code the moment it's created.

  • Every tournament gets a short code and a QR code you can print, post, or drop into a WhatsApp group.
  • Anyone with the link can view live matches, the bracket or points table, and the schedule - no sign-up or app install required.
  • Only the organizer can edit; everyone else gets a read-only, always-current view.
  • Works equally well pinned to a noticeboard at the ground or shared in a team chat.

Where the share link lives

Share links open directly in the Sportik app at https://app.sportik.in/tournament/<code> - the same place organizers manage the tournament from, just in read-only mode for everyone else.
