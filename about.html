About Sportik

Sportik started from a simple problem: running a local sports tournament usually means a spreadsheet for fixtures, a WhatsApp group for the draw, and a printed sheet at the ground for the bracket - three different tools that all fall out of sync the moment something changes.

Sportik replaces all three with one app: pick a format, run the draw live, generate fixtures, and share a single link that stays up to date for every player and fan - no one else needs to install anything or log in to see it.

We're a small team building for organizers who run tournaments on weekends, not for a living - so the app stays free to organize, and free tools like the coin toss, picker wheel, and budget calculator are available even without installing it.
