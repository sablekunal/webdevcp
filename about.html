Building tools for grassroots sports organizers

Running a local tournament shouldn't require three broken spreadsheets, an unruly WhatsApp group, and printed sheets taped to a fence.

Why Sportik exists

Sportik started from a simple, frustrating reality: running a local sports tournament usually means a spreadsheet for fixtures, a WhatsApp group for the draw, and a printed sheet at the ground for the bracket. Three different tools that all fall out of sync the moment a rain delay hits or a team drops out.

Sportik replaces all three with one single, intuitive app: pick a format, run the live draw in front of captains, auto-generate fixtures without scheduling clashes, and share a single real-time link. Every player, coach, and fan stays up to date without installing anything or logging in.

We are a focused team building specifically for organizers who run tournaments on weekends out of pure passion, not for a living. That's why the app is free to organize, and free utilities like the coin toss, picker wheel, and budget calculator are accessible right here in your browser.
